# User Stories

## Quan ly phong

- As a chu tro, I want tao phong voi ma phong duy nhat, so nguoi toi da va gia mac dinh, so that toi co the quan ly danh sach phong cho thue.
- As a chu tro, I want loc phong theo trang thai va cong no, so that toi nhanh chong biet phong nao can xu ly.
- As a chu tro, I want ngung su dung phong thay vi xoa cung khi da co giao dich, so that lich su tai chinh khong bi mat.

## Quan ly nguoi thue

- As a chu tro, I want them nguoi thue va tim kiem theo ten, so dien thoai hoac CCCD, so that toi tra cuu nhanh thong tin.
- As a chu tro, I want gan nhieu nguoi thue vao mot phong, so that toi quan ly dung thuc te phong co nhieu nguoi o.
- As a chu tro, I want chuyen nguoi thue sang phong khac va giu lich su, so that toi truy vet duoc qua trinh o.

## Tenancy

- As a chu tro, I want tao tenancy rieng cho moi lan thue, so that lich su gia, nguoi o va chu ky thanh toan khong bi ghi de.
- As a chu tro, I want luu gia va chu ky thanh toan tai thoi diem bat dau, so that viec doi gia sau nay khong lam sai lich su.

## Tien phong va thanh toan

- As a chu tro, I want ghi nhan thanh toan tien phong theo ngay, tuan hoac thang, so that `paidUntil` duoc cap nhat dung.
- As a chu tro, I want ghi nhan thanh toan mot phan, so that cong no con lai duoc theo doi ma khong tang ngay da thanh toan khi chua du tien.
- As a chu tro, I want huy thanh toan bang nghiep vu co audit, so that so lieu tai chinh co the duoc tinh lai va truy vet.

## Dien nuoc

- As a chu tro, I want cau hinh gia dien nuoc mac dinh va gia rieng theo phong/hop dong, so that moi hoa don dung gia ap dung.
- As a chu tro, I want nhap chi so dien nuoc hang thang, so that he thong tinh tien theo tieu thu thuc te.
- As a chu tro, I want cron tao hoa don dien nuoc cuoi thang khong trung, so that toi khong phai tao thu cong cho tat ca phong.

## Hoa don va cong no

- As a chu tro, I want xem hoa don voi cac dong tien chi tiet, so that toi giai thich duoc tong tien cho nguoi thue.
- As a chu tro, I want xem danh sach phong con no va muc canh bao, so that toi uu tien nhac no.
- As a chu tro, I want ghi nhan thanh toan tu trang cong no, so that viec thu tien nhanh va dung ngu canh.

## Dashboard va bao cao

- As a chu tro, I want dashboard tong quan ve phong, doanh thu, cong no va canh bao, so that toi nam duoc tinh hinh nha tro.
- As a chu tro, I want xuat CSV bao cao doanh thu va thanh toan, so that toi co the luu tru hoac doi chieu ben ngoai.

## Cau hinh va audit

- As a chu tro, I want cau hinh ngay chot, ngay den han, tien te, mui gio va lam tron, so that he thong tinh tien nhat quan.
- As a chu tro, I want xem audit log cac thao tac quan trong, so that toi truy vet thay doi khi co sai lech.
